# Competitive Landscape and Risks (FY2025 10-K)
Source: `research/sec/filings/10-K_2025-10-31_aapl-20250927.htm`

## Competitors by Product Line (described qualitatively in 10-K)
- iPhone: highly competitive smartphone market with aggressive pricing and rapid product cycles.
- Mac: personal computer market with frequent product introductions and price sensitivity.
- iPad: tablet market with evolving standards and short product life cycles.
- Wearables/Home/Accessories: fast innovation cycles and intense competition on features and price.
- Services: competition from digital content platforms, payment providers, and cloud services.

## Platform and Regulatory Risks (local evidence + inference)
- App Store: key distribution platform for apps and digital content (business model dependence).
- Ecosystem lock-in: services depend on device installed base and platform stickiness.
- Privacy: product positioning often emphasizes security/privacy; regulatory changes can affect services.
- Note: specific regulatory actions are not documented in local sources and require external verification.

## Supply Chain and Component Risks
- Component supply can be constrained; some components are sourced from single or limited suppliers.
- The Company competes with other OEMs for critical components.
- Purchase obligations and forecasting errors can lead to excess/shortages and margin pressure.

## Currency and Macro
- Geographic segmentation implies exposure to regional macro cycles and FX volatility.
- This is an inference from the global segment mix; confirm with latest risk factors when available.

## Exposure Highlights (FY2025 net sales)
- Services represented 26.23% of total net sales.
- Greater China represented 15.47% of net sales and declined 4.95% YoY vs FY2024.
