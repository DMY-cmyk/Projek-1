# Business Overview Notes (FY2025 10-K)
Source: `research/sec/filings/10-K_2025-10-31_aapl-20250927.htm`

## Segments
- Products: iPhone; Mac (personal computers); tablets (iPad); wearables; home devices (Apple TV, HomePod); accessories.
- Services: Advertising; AppleCare; Cloud Services; Digital Content (App Store, Apple Arcade, Fitness+, Apple Music, News+, TV+); Payment Services (Apple Card, Apple Pay).

FY2025 net sales by product (USD billions):
- iPhone: 209.586
- Mac: 33.708
- iPad: 28.023
- Wearables, Home and Accessories: 35.686
- Services: 109.158
Total: 416.161

## Geography (Reportable Segments)
- Americas: North and South America.
- Europe: Europe plus India, Middle East, Africa.
- Greater China: China mainland, Hong Kong, Taiwan.
- Japan: Japan.
- Rest of Asia Pacific: Australia, New Zealand, and other Asia-Pacific countries not in the segments above.

FY2025 net sales by geography (USD billions):
- Americas: 178.353 (42.86%)

Total: 416.161

## Distribution and Ecosystem
- Customers span consumer, small/mid-size business, education, enterprise, and government markets.
- Direct channels: retail stores, online stores, and direct sales force.
- Indirect channels: carriers and other resellers for products and some services.
- 2025 net sales mix: 40% direct, 60% indirect.
- Services are sold via Apple service platforms and reinforce device ecosystem usage.

## Strategy Themes (from Business + Competition + R&D sections)
- Pricing: markets are highly competitive with aggressive pricing and consumer price sensitivity.
- Innovation: competitiveness depends on continual flow of new products/services and ongoing R&D.
- Supply chain: component supply can be constrained; some components are single/limited source.
- Services expansion: growing portfolio of subscriptions, content, cloud, payments, and support.
