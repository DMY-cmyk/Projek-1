# Thesis and Scenarios (Draft)
Baseline period: FY2021-FY2025 financials in `data/`

## Bull Case
- Drivers: services mix expansion, new device cycles, improved monetization of installed base.
- Assumptions: mid-to-high single-digit revenue growth, modest margin expansion, stable capital returns.
- Catalysts: stronger upgrade cycles, higher services attach, successful new form factors.

## Base Case
- Normalized growth and margins: low single-digit revenue growth with operating margin roughly stable around FY2025 levels.
- Capital allocation: continued buybacks and modest dividend growth.

## Bear Case
- Downside drivers: slower device demand, intensified price competition, supply constraints.
- Stress points: revenue growth stalls or turns negative; margins compress from pricing pressure and component costs.

## KPIs to Monitor
- Total revenue growth and YoY trends.
- Gross margin and operating margin stability.
- Services revenue mix and growth rate.
- iPhone-related revenue cycle strength.
- Capital return pace (buybacks + dividends).
