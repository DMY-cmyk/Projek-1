# DCF Base Case (Illustrative)

Assumptions (FY 2025 baseline):
- Revenue (FY 2025): 416.161 (USD billions)
- Revenue growth (Y1-Y5): 3% annually
- EBIT margin: 31.5%
- Tax rate: 15%
- Depreciation & amortization: 2.8% of revenue
- Capex: 3.0% of revenue
- Change in net working capital: 0.2% of revenue
- WACC: 8.0%
- Terminal growth: 2.5%

- PV of terminal value: 1613.911 (USD billions)
- Enterprise value: 2091.043 (USD billions)
